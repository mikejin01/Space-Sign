## Decisions to confirm before starting

Each one has a recommended default. The steps below assume it.

1. **Where Permits and Violations link to.** *Recommended:* a new homepage section, "Permits & Violations", with two panels anchored at `#permits` and `#violations`. The copy comes from the client's own site (`BUSINESS-INFO.md` → Permits & Violations). *Alternative:* two separate pages. That needs routing, which this single-page app doesn't have, so it is a structural change and would go through the full organization process (4 options + review) first.
2. **Storefront photo.** No photo of Space Sign's own shop exists in `public/assets` (only logos and client work). *Recommended:* ask the client for a landscape photo of the College Point storefront, at least 1600px wide. Until it arrives, the section shows a labeled placeholder. Don't use a Google Street View capture, because Google's terms don't allow reusing that imagery on a website.
3. **Phone number in the mobile menu.** *Recommended:* keep it, as tap-to-call is valuable on phones. The client's note was about the desktop top bar. It still gets the shared format (A6).
4. **Marquee direction.** *Recommended:* follow the request literally, so the logos travel left → right. Reversing it is a one-line change.
5. **Business hours.** Mon–Fri 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, Sat–Sun closed. This was confirmed on the Yahoo Local listing, and search summaries of the Yelp and Bluebook listings agree (those pages block direct fetching). I could not check the Google listing directly, so Step 8 includes a check against Google Maps. If Google differs, use Google's hours, since the client said "find on google".
6. **Hero after removing Sandobe.** *Recommended:* keep 3 slides rather than adding a replacement image.
7. **Other three designs and the switcher.** Out of scope for this update; see Follow-ups.
